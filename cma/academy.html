About CMA Academy Asia

Empowering Organisations Through Learning & Development

CMA Academy Asia is the training and development arm of CMA Group Sdn. Bhd. Based in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, we deliver practical, results-driven corporate training programs designed to build capacity, enhance productivity and drive sustainable growth for organisations across Malaysia and the region.

Our trainers combine deep industry experience with proven adult-learning methodologies, ensuring every session is engaging, relevant and immediately applicable to the workplace.
