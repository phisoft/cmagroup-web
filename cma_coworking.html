CMA KK Coworking Space

A Collaborative Workspace in Kota Kinabalu

Located at KK Times Square Office, our coworking space is designed for entrepreneurs, freelancers, startups, and growing teams. Whether you need a hot desk for the day, a private office for your team, or a training space for your next workshop — we have flexible solutions to fit your needs.

With modern amenities, high-speed internet, meeting rooms, and a supportive community, CMA KK Coworking Space is more than just a place to work — it's a space to grow your business.

Our Spaces & Services

Flexible Solutions For Every Need

From short-term rentals to long-term memberships, we offer a variety of workspace solutions to suit your business.

Hot Desk

Flexible seating in our open workspace area. Perfect for freelancers and remote workers who need a productive environment without a long-term commitment.

Private Office

Fully furnished private offices for teams who need dedicated, secure space. Ideal for startups and growing companies looking for a professional base.

Training Space

A versatile space designed for corporate training, workshops, and seminars. Equipped with presentation facilities to support engaging learning sessions.

Meeting Room

Professional meeting rooms for client discussions, team huddles, or board meetings. Bookable by the hour or for half-day / full-day sessions.

Training Open Space

A spacious open area for larger training sessions, team-building activities, and group workshops. Perfect for interactive and hands-on learning.

Virtual Office Solutions

Establish a professional business presence with our virtual office package — including a prime business address, mail handling, and meeting room access.

Interview Venue

Conduct professional interviews in a comfortable, private setting. Ideal for HR teams and recruiters who need a neutral venue for candidate sessions.

Caterings

Enhance your event or training session with our catering services. From light refreshments to full meals, we can arrange food for your booking.

Membership

Join our coworking community with flexible membership plans. Enjoy access to hot desks, meeting rooms, and networking opportunities with like-minded professionals.
