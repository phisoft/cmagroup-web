About CMA Academy Asia

Founded 2021, Reimagined 2025

Founded in 2021, CMA Academy Asia was built on the belief that corporate training should be as engaging as it is informative. As we transitioned to CMA Academy Asia Sdn. Bhd. in 2025, we took the opportunity to move beyond our traditional roots. While we remain deeply committed to our core expertise in accounting and finance, we have grown to meet the demands of a changing workforce. Today, our reach spans across sales and marketing, leadership, communication, personal development, and compliance, with a renewed focus on human resources and sales communication.

We've always felt that the best way to learn is by doing. That's why we take technical or complex subjects and turn them into accessible, hands-on experiences. By weaving behavioral tools like DISC, MBTI, and NLP into our simulations and gamified learning, we help participants truly connect with the material. Whether we are working with a government-linked organization or a team in hospitality, engineering, or education, our goal is to make sure every learner walks away with practical skills they can use immediately.

Our team of certified trainers is dedicated to more than just ticking boxes. We are here to support the long-term growth of your people and the lasting success of your organization. At CMA Academy Asia, we believe that when you empower a workforce with the right mix of technical knowledge and communication skills, the impact is measurable and profound.

Vision & Mission

What Drives Us

Vision

To be the catalyst for transformational change, shaping the future of corporates and individuals through transformative learning experiences that drive innovation, growth, and prosperity.

Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, innovative training programs that inspire growth, foster leadership, and enhance skills for both corporates and individuals. We are committed to delivering exceptional learning experiences that transform lives and businesses, ultimately contributing to a more skilled and empowered workforce.

Innovative Training

The focus is on delivering training programs that are high quality and also innovative, ensuring that they are engaging and effective in meeting the learning objectives.

This includes case studies discussions, brainstorming and group discussions, practice through presentations, gamified learning and creative experiential learning methods that are guaranteed to make the training fun and effective.

Transform Lives & Business

The ultimate aim is to bring about positive change in the lives of individuals and the success of businesses, through the skills and knowledge gained from the training programs.

By providing high-quality training programs, the mission is to contribute to the development of a workforce that is skilled, empowered, and capable of driving innovation and growth in their organizations.

Our Competitive Advantage

Why Choose Us

ACCOUNTING, BUSINESS & FINANCIAL LITERACY PROGRAM

CMA Academy Asia stands out due to its team of industry experts who have extensive experience in finance, accounting, and financial planning. Their trainers have theoretical knowledge and also practical experience working in the financial industry. This gives them a unique perspective and allows them to provide real-world examples and case studies that resonate with participants.

Moreover, they go beyond traditional classroom-style training by offering hands-on workshops and simulations. Participants have the opportunity to apply their learning in practical scenarios, such as creating a budget, understanding cashflows and net worth, or designing their own retirement plan. This hands-on approach reinforces learning and also increases participants' confidence in managing their finances effectively. By leveraging their industry expertise, practical approach, and commitment to staying current, CMA Academy Asia provides a learning experience that is engaging, informative, and highly valuable to participants, giving them a competitive edge in the market.
